Small Actions, Big Impact

Simple changes in your daily routine can lead to massive environmental benefits.

🌳

Plant a Tree

Each tree absorbs up to 22 kg of CO2 annually, improving air quality.

🚆

Use Public Transport

Cuts individual CO2 emissions by 50% per trip compared to driving.

🚰

Reusable Bottle

Prevents 83g of plastic waste per use, reducing landfill impact.

🍃

Compost Food Scraps

Lowers methane emissions by diverting waste from landfills.

💡

Switch to LED Bulbs

Consumes 80% less energy than traditional bulbs, saving power.

🛍️

Cloth Bags

Prevents over 150 plastic bags from polluting the environment yearly.

🥗

Reduce Meat Consumption

Lowers your carbon footprint by up to 2.5 tons annually.

🔌

Unplug Devices

Saves up to 10% of household energy by avoiding phantom power.

🚲

Cycle Short Distances

Eliminates emissions entirely for trips under 5 km.
