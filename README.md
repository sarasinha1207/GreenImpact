# GreenImpact

GreenImpact is an AI-powered platform that helps users track and reduce their carbon footprint. The platform combines smart tracking, personalized sustainability plans, and gamification to make sustainable living engaging and rewarding. With the combination of carbon credit marketplaces, 3D Earth heatmap visualization, getting weather conditions based on user location, and community hub for like minded peers.

## Inspiration

The climate crisis represents one of the greatest challenges of our generation. While many individuals want to make a difference, they often lack:
1. Tools to accurately measure their environmental impact
2. Personalized guidance for sustainable living
3. Visible proof that their actions matter

GreenImpact was born from the belief that technology can bridge this gap by making sustainability accessible, measurable, and rewarding for everyone.

## Features

### 1. Carbon Footprint Tracking & AI-Based Sustainability Plan
- Tracks user activities such as transportation, energy use, and food habits to calculate carbon footprint.
- Provides category wise breakdown and historical trends for better decision making.
- AI generates personalized sustainability plans based on user behavior.
- Suggests specific actions to reduce carbon emissions with real-time insights.
- Enables progress tracking with data-driven analytics for continuous improvement.

### 2. Gamification & Leaderboards
- Users complete sustainability challenges and daily tasks.
- Earn points, unlock achievements, and rank on leaderboards.
- Encourages eco-friendly habits through interactive engagement.

### 3. Carbon Credit & Offsetting Marketplace
- Provides a transparent system for investing in carbon offset projects.
- Blockchain-based verification of carbon credits.
- Real-time tracking of investments and their environmental impact.

### 4. User Activity Tracking
- Monitors user activities such as transportation, energy consumption, and lifestyle habits.
- Helps analyze user behavior to understand carbon footprint patterns.
- Enables AI-driven personalized recommendations for effective sustainability improvements.


### 5. Community Hub
- **Community Space:** A dedicated platform for users to share experiences, insights, and sustainable practices.

- **Challenges & Activities:** Engaging group challenges to promote collaborative sustainability efforts.

- **Expert Insights:** Webinars and articles from environmental specialists to educate users on reducing carbon footprints.


## Google Technologies Used in GreenImpact  
GreenImpact leverages several Google technologies to deliver accurate, intelligent, and engaging features while keeping the project lightweight and privacy-focused:

  **1. Google Gemini API**  
  The core AI engine behind personalized sustainability insights. Gemini analyzes user-logged activities (processed locally) to generate tailored, practical recommendations (e.g., "Switch short commutes to cycling" or "Try one plant-based meal this week"). This makes complex climate science simple and actionable for everyday users.  
  
 **2. Google Fonts (Inter & Outfit)**  
  Used for a clean, modern, and highly readable typography across the entire dashboard, ensuring a professional and consistent user interface on all devices.  
  
 **3. Google Charts / Chart.js Integration**  
  While Chart.js is the primary library for rendering interactive doughnut and line charts (emissions breakdown and weekly trends), the design and responsiveness are optimized for seamless integration with Google ecosystem standards.  
  
 **4. Google-Hosted CDN Services**  
Tailwind CSS and Chart.js are loaded via Google's CDN infrastructure (jsDelivr, backed by Google), ensuring fast and reliable delivery worldwide.  


These technologies enable GreenImpact to be smart, visually appealing, and accessible — all while maintaining the project's educational and privacy-first approach. 🌿

## Future Development  

1. Enhanced AI Capabilities  
- Integrate predictive modeling to forecast future emissions based on user habits.  
- Provide adaptive recommendations that evolve with seasonal changes, festivals, and local events.

2. Expanded Activity Tracking
- Include shopping, water usage, and waste management for a more holistic footprint.
- Integrate smart home and IoT devices to automatically track energy consumption.

3. Mobile App & Cross-Platform Support
- Develop native Android and iOS apps for seamless on-the-go tracking.
- Enable offline logging and synchronization for low-connectivity regions.

4. Localized Recommendations & Education
- Include hyper-local tips for Indian cities (e.g., pollution alerts, seasonal diet suggestions).
- Add educational modules about climate change, health impact, and sustainable alternatives.
